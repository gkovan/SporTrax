A little competition.
A lot of rallies.

Tug of War turns hitting sessions into a no-serve mini game, saved in your activity history.

How to play Tug of War
  1. Start at 5. Player 1 pulls toward 10; player 2 pulls toward 0.
  2. Let the ball cross the net twice before a point can score.
  3. A point for player 1 adds one. A point for player 2 subtracts one.
  4. Reach your end of the score to win.
